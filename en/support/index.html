Support

Need help with the app? We're here.

Email us: support@navicoreapp.com
We typically respond within 24–48 hours on business days.

What to include in your message

A few details help us answer faster. Please mention whichever apply to your situation:

Account & sign-in

  • Your nickname or NAV-XXX identifier (visible in Profile)
  • The email used to register, or whether you signed in with Apple / Google / as a guest
  • A short description of what went wrong

Subscription & billing

  • The Apple ID email associated with the purchase
  • Which product you bought (monthly or annual)
  • Approximate purchase date

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel a subscription, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. We cannot cancel or refund directly — Apple handles all billing operations.

Bug reports

  • iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • Device model
  • What you were doing when the issue appeared
  • Screenshot if possible

Privacy & data

You can export your data or permanently delete your account directly inside the app: Profile → Export my data / Profile → Delete account. For other privacy questions, email privacy@navicoreapp.com and see our Privacy Policy.

Content & chat reports

To report inappropriate behaviour in chat, long-press the message inside the app and choose Report. Our moderators review reports continuously. For urgent matters or appeals of a moderation decision, email us.
